JARVIS PWA - upload ALL files in this folder to the root of your GitHub Pages repo.

Wake word setup:
1. Make a free account at https://console.picovoice.ai and copy your AccessKey.
2. Open the app > Config > paste it into 'Picovoice AccessKey' > Save.
3. Tap Wake, allow the mic, and say "Jarvis".

Optional "Friday" wake word:
1. In Picovoice Console > Porcupine, train the phrase "Friday" (English), platform: Web (WASM).
2. Download the .ppn file, rename it to friday.ppn, and upload it next to index.html.
